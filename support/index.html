Common questions

My notifications aren't arriving

Check these in order:

  1. In iOS Settings → Notifications → Støtte, make sure notifications are allowed.
  2. In Støtte, tap the bell and confirm notifications are on and at least one day has morning or afternoon turned on.
  3. Check whether a Focus mode (Do Not Disturb, Sleep, and so on) is silencing the app.
  4. Open Støtte once. It refreshes your upcoming notifications every time you open it, so it's worth opening the app at least every week or two.
How do I change or turn off alternate names?

Open Settings (the gear), then Alternative Names. You can switch individual names on or off, add your own, or use the master switch to turn all of them off. Turning the master switch off keeps your choices, so they're there when you switch it back on.

Why did my message history reset?

If you've been away from the app for more than 15 minutes, Støtte starts fresh with a new greeting. Message history only lives in memory while the app is running; it isn't saved, and that's on purpose.

Why doesn't the greeting match the time of day?

The greeting is chosen when you open the app, and it refreshes after you've been away. If you leave the app open across the morning-to-afternoon boundary, it will update the next time you come back to it.

How do I delete my data?

Everything Støtte stores (your name, birthday, and settings) lives only on your phone. Deleting the app removes all of it. There's nothing on a server to delete.

Something else?

Bug reports and ideas are welcome. If you can, include your iPhone model and iOS version.
