Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version: the Støtte app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

The app

What Støtte stores on your device

  • The name you want to be called, any alternate names you add, and your birthday if you choose to enter one.
  • Your notification settings, such as which days and times get a message.

This information is saved in the app's local storage on your phone. It is never sent to me or to anyone else. Støtte has no accounts and no servers.

Notifications

Støtte schedules its notifications on your phone using iOS. It does not use push notification servers. The text of each message is prepared on your device, using your name if you've set one. iOS asks for your permission before the app can send notifications, and you can change that at any time in iOS Settings.

Message history

The messages you flip through are kept in memory only while the app is running. They are not saved to storage and are gone when the app closes or resets.

What Støtte does not do

  • No analytics.
  • No crash reporting or third-party SDKs.
  • No advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No data sold or shared, because there is no data to share.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and everything it stored is removed from your device.

Apple

If you share app analytics or crash data with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide anonymous reports to me through App Store Connect. That is Apple's system, governed by Apple's privacy policy, and I can't identify you from it. If you install Støtte through TestFlight, Apple's TestFlight terms also apply.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

This website

This site (stotte.me) is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information about visitors, such as IP address and browser type, under GitHub's privacy statement. I do not run analytics, set cookies, or load fonts, scripts, or images from third parties on this site, and I don't have access to GitHub's logs of your visit.

If you email hello@stotte.me, I'll receive your email address and whatever you write, and I'll use it only to reply to you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I'll update the date at the top of this page and note the change in the changelog.

Contact

Questions: hello@stotte.me
