Good morning, Sam. You showed up today, and that counts.

An example of the kind of message Støtte shows.

Støtte is Danish and Norwegian for “support.” It's an iPhone app that gives you a short, kind message when you open it and sends a few gentle notifications through the day.

Coming soon to the App Store
  • Encouraging messages Tap the gift button for another one. Swipe back to find one you liked.
  • Gentle notifications Choose which days get a morning or afternoon message. Times shift a little each week so they never feel like an alarm.
  • Made for you Add your name, your birthday, and any nicknames you like. All of it stays on your phone.

No account. No ads. No tracking. Read the privacy policy.
